π PiViet - Chinh sach Bao mat

Cap nhat: 24/11/2026

1. PiViet la gi?

PiViet la app ho tro Pioneer Viet Nam viet bai, tao noi dung ve Pi Network. App chi chay tren trinh duyet, khong thu thap thong tin nhay cam.

2. Thong tin thu thap

Khi ban dang nhap bang Pi Auth, app chi nhan username va uid tu Pi Platform qua App Studio de hien thi ten cua ban. App khong luu mat khau, khong luu vi Pi, khong truy cap danh ba, anh, tin nhan.

3. Cach su dung

Thong tin ban nhap (tieu de bai viet, noi dung) chi luu tren trinh duyet cua ban (localStorage), khong gui ve may chu cua chung toi, tru khi ban tu copy va chia se.

4. Ben thu ba

App su dung Pi SDK (sdk.minepi.com) va App Studio backend de xac thuc. Vui long xem chinh sach cua Pi Network.

5. Lien he

Lien he nha phat trien: qua GitHub https://github.com/watashichin-design/piviet - Chinh Quang Nguyen

PiViet tuan thu Pi App Guidelines. Chi ho tro Pioneer, khong mua ban Pi lay tien mat.
